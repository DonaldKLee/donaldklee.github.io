# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, fellow builders, and curious visitors landing on Donald Lee's
personal site, usually for a couple of minutes and often on a phone. They want
a fast read on who he is and what he's made, and they should leave having
enjoyed the visit.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio for Donald Lee: a self-taught developer at UBC, currently
interning at Amazon. It introduces him, shows his work and experience, and makes
it easy to reach out. Success is a visitor who explores past the hero and
remembers the site (and Donald) afterward.

## Brand Personality
Playful, warm, and quietly capable. The conceit: the whole site is "a rendered
markdown file" (`donaldlee.xyz`), which fits a builder of AI/dev tooling. Three
words: friendly, crafted, curious. It should feel like a fun thing to poke at,
not a résumé to read.

## Anti-references
- Heavy, dark "developer terminal" portfolios.
- Dense résumé walls of text.
- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero-metric template, identical icon-card grids).
- Editorial-magazine serif-and-drop-cap treatments.

## Design Principles
- **Flow over pages.** One scroll, each section handing off to the next. Short
  attention spans win.
- **One playful anchor.** The 3D Mii carries personality so the rest can stay
  minimal.
- **Practice the concept.** The markdown/chatbot framing shows, not tells, what
  Donald builds.
- **Earn the motion.** Movement is part of the build, never decoration for its
  own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 on white. Every animation has a
`prefers-reduced-motion` path (loader, smooth scroll, reveals, and the Mii's
float all degrade to static/instant). 3D canvas has descriptive alt text.
